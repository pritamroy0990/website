WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

Draft policy for review and customization before this website is published.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This draft explains the intended privacy practices for the Breath Of Kindness Services website. It must be reviewed and adapted to the actual business, hosting provider, form processing, analytics, and applicable laws before publication.

Information you provide

If you contact the business or request an estimate, you may choose to provide your name, email address, phone number, project location, service interests, preferred timing, and project description. Do not send sensitive personal information through a general inquiry.

How information may be used

Information submitted to the business may be used to respond to inquiries, discuss project scope, prepare estimates, schedule services, and maintain business records. The business should only use personal information for legitimate purposes explained to the customer.

How this starter website handles the estimate form

The included estimate form validates fields in your browser and prepares an email using your email application. This starter implementation does not send form data to a website server or store it in a database. If a form provider, analytics tool, CRM, or file-upload service is added, this policy must be updated to explain that provider and its data practices.

Cookies, analytics, and third-party services

This starter website does not include analytics or advertising scripts. It loads fonts and photographs from third-party services, which may receive technical request information such as IP address and browser details. If analytics, embedded maps, chat, or marketing tools are added, disclose their use and provide consent controls where legally required.

Data sharing and retention

Do not sell or share personal information except as disclosed and permitted by applicable law. Define retention periods, access controls, and procedures for deletion requests before launch. Business communications may be retained as needed to manage requests, legal obligations, and business operations.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information. Add the verified business contact details and any required jurisdiction-specific disclosures before publication.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website functionality or business practices change. The effective date should be updated when revisions are published.

Launch requirement: This is a starter draft, not legal advice. Have the final policy reviewed for the business's jurisdiction and actual data handling.
